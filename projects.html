CASE № 01
CAT · QUALITY ASSURANCE
STATION · GCGV

Product quality grading across finished polymers & glycols.

Situation

ExxonMobil's GCGV complex required reliable product quality decisions for finished polymers and glycols across multiple production lines.

Task

Serve as Product Quality Grader, providing go/no-go decisions for finished product against internal and customer specifications.

Action

Implemented systematic verification against specs, coordinated with operations and business on grading decisions, escalated deviations through proper channels.

Result

Maintained consistent product-release standards across shifts, contributing to continuous on-spec shipment of polymer and glycol product lines.

CASE № 02
CAT · EMERGENCY RESPONSE
CERT · FAA-107

EHS drone operations for aerial incident assessment.

Situation

Emergency response and industrial hygiene events required rapid assessment of areas that were difficult or dangerous to access on foot.

Task

Support EHS drone operations as an FAA Part 107 certified pilot, providing aerial assessment during incidents and maintenance activities.

Action

Deployed UAS for perimeter checks, air-monitoring support, and visual assessment of incident zones. Coordinated with emergency response and EHS teams.

Result

Contributed to safer incident management by reducing the need for personnel in hazardous zones during active response scenarios.

CASE № 03
CAT · SAFETY
ROLE · T/A ADVISOR

Turnaround safety training advisory, zero incidents in scope.

Situation

Major turnarounds require close coordination between lab and field activities with strict safety protocols across multiple work groups.

Task

Contribute as T/A Safety Training Advisor, reinforcing safe work practices and aligning lab activities with field execution.

Action

Facilitated safety briefings, monitored protocol compliance during turnaround activities, and coordinated between lab teams and field crews to resolve conflicts.

Result

Supported successful turnaround execution with zero safety incidents recorded in areas of responsibility.

CASE № 04
CAT · OPERATIONS
STATION · BV

Third-party QA across crude, ULSD, gasoline, VGO, VLSFO, jet fuel.

Situation

Bureau Veritas served multiple refineries requiring third-party quality verification across a wide range of petroleum products under tight timelines.

Task

Execute standardized test methods and communicate results to refinery and marine customers in time-sensitive cargo and custody-transfer situations.

Action

Conducted testing across crude oils, ULSD, gasoline, VGO, VLSFO, and jet fuel. Prepared documentation and communicated results accurately under pressure.

Result

Maintained compliance with client specifications and regulatory requirements across all engagements during the assignment period.

CASE № 05
CAT · LEADERSHIP
SHIFT · NIGHTS

Night-shift lab POC, primary decision hub when supervision is limited.

Situation

Night-shift laboratory operations at GCGV required a reliable POC to coordinate priorities and communicate across operations, engineering, and business teams.

Task

Serve as primary lab POC on shift, managing sample priorities, communicating results, and making real-time coordination decisions.

Action

Directed sample prioritization based on production needs, communicated results, escalated issues, and maintained consistent operations through off-hours.

Result

Established a reputation as a reliable decision-maker on shift, contributing to smooth operations and timely product release.
